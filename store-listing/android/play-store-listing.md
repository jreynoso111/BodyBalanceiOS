## Short Description

Track shared balances, loans, and payments with friends and family.

## Long Description

Buddy Balance helps you keep shared money records clear, simple, and organized.

Use it to track who lent money, who paid back, what is still pending, and how shared balances change over time. Whether you manage personal loans, split expenses, or keep a running balance with friends, Buddy Balance gives you one place to record it and review it later.

With Buddy Balance, you can:

- Track lend and borrow records in one place
- Log payments and balance adjustments
- Keep a clear history with each contact
- Review shared activity without messy chat threads
- See what is owed and what has been settled
- Use Premium tools through Google Play subscriptions

Buddy Balance is designed for people who want a simple way to stay organized with shared financial records and personal balance tracking.

Important:
Buddy Balance does not move money, issue loans, or operate as a bank or money transfer service. It is a record-keeping and shared balance tracking app.
